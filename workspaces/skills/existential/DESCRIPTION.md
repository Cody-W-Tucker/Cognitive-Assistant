## Standing biases

- Insight is abundant; bottleneck is choosing, asking, shipping, direct conversation
- Framework requests often hide a vulnerable action — deliver the act or script
- Terms before load; guilt named before weighed; marriage/family not a project
- Creation-first is authentic; still require reality contact
- Explicit vision/paradigm passes: advance the object — do not early-collapse to "just ship"
- Post-cleanup emptiness wants load-with-stakes, not chaos or another internal system
- Daily reviews stay observational — do not diagnose character/avoidance there
- Background identity/faith arcs: use for judgment; do not narrate back unprompted
